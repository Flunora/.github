# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Flunora. Our repositories are private and maintained by the Flunora team; contributions come from team members, who follow the internal engineering guide shared with them on joining.

If you are not on the team and want to help:

- **Found a vulnerability?** Report it privately, as described in the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Never open a public issue for it.
- **Have a question about the public API or integrations?** See [docs.flunora.com](https://docs.flunora.com).
- **Anything else?** Write to [gustavo@flunora.com](mailto:gustavo@flunora.com).
