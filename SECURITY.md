# Security policy

Flunora handles commercial and personal data on behalf of our customers, and we take its protection seriously. We appreciate reports from anyone who finds a weakness in our products.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email [gustavo@flunora.com](mailto:gustavo@flunora.com) with the subject `Security report`. Please do not open a GitHub issue, discussion or pull request, and do not share the details publicly until we have confirmed a fix.

A useful report includes:

- the affected product or URL (for example `app.flunora.com` or `docs.flunora.com`);
- a description of the issue and its impact;
- the steps to reproduce it, with requests and responses where relevant;
- any proof of concept, kept to the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue.

## What to expect

We acknowledge every report within two business days, give an initial assessment within five business days, and keep you informed until the issue is resolved. When a fix ships we are happy to credit you, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope and rules

In scope: the Flunora web application, its public API and the public documentation site.

While researching, please:

- use only accounts you own or have explicit permission to test;
- never access, modify or delete data that is not yours, and stop as soon as you reach another customer's data;
- avoid anything that degrades the service, such as load tests, denial of service or automated scanning at high volume;
- do not use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks.

Research done in good faith and within these rules will not lead to legal action from Flunora.

## Supported versions

Flunora is delivered as a hosted service and only the version in production is supported. Fixes are deployed continuously; customers do not need to take any action unless we contact them.
