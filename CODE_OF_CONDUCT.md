# Code of conduct

Flunora is built by a small team that works closely together. We expect everyone who takes part in our repositories, reviews and discussions to help keep that collaboration respectful and productive.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and decisions, never people.
- Be clear and specific in reviews: say what is wrong, why it matters and how to fix it.
- Share context generously. Write down the reasons behind decisions so the next person does not have to guess.
- Respect confidentiality. Customer data, credentials and internal information never leave the systems they belong to, including in screenshots, issues and chats.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing anyone's private information without permission, in any form.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, write to [gustavo@flunora.com](mailto:gustavo@flunora.com). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers may take any action they find appropriate, up to removing someone from the organization.

This code applies to all Flunora repositories and to every space where Flunora work happens.
