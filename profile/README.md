Flunora brings meetings, emails, messages, pipeline and the customer base into a single source of context. Our AI, Aurora, reads that context and shows the risk, the forecast and the next step of every deal, always with the evidence behind each conclusion. Sales and customer success teams work from the same history, from the first prospecting touch to renewal and expansion.

Flunora is built in Brazil, for Brazilian companies.

## What we build

**Prospecting.** Company dossiers read from the web, with a source and a confidence level on every field. Leads from forms, webhooks, Meta Lead Ads, spreadsheets and email, with the origin of each one kept for attribution.

**Deals.** Pipelines where meetings turn into data: decision criteria, objections, competitors and risks are recorded on the deal next to the words that support them. Risks surface with their reason before they become a loss.

**Customer success.** When a deal closes, the account carries its full history into onboarding, health, renewal and expansion.

**Aurora.** Deal reviews against the company's own playbook, context prepared before every meeting, and actions taken within the permissions of the person who asks, with approval required for anything that leaves the platform.

**Integrations.** WhatsApp, Gmail and Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and Teams, meeting recording, Slack, HubSpot, Pipedrive, ClickUp and Notion.

## How we build

We hold a small number of principles above any single feature.

**Evidence over assertion.** Every AI conclusion links to its source, and every number on a screen can be traced to the records it came from.

**Privacy by design.** Every change that touches personal data answers, before it ships, what data it is, its purpose and legal basis under the LGPD, who can read it, when it is deleted, whether it leaves the platform and how its owner can see, export or erase it.

**Least privilege by default.** Each person sees only what their role allows, including in reports and AI answers.

**Simple, verified changes.** Small diffs, typed end to end, checked by automated gates before they reach production.
